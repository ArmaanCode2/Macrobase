MacroBase built-in food catalog: data sources

Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
  Vijayakumar et al., Current Developments in Nutrition 8(7):103790, 2024.
  https://github.com/lindsayjaacks/Indian-Nutrient-Databank-INDB-
  Published without a licence; MacroBase uses it with this credit.
  Recipes and their nutrition per 100 g. Fried foods, soups and some portions are corrected in the
  catalog, some with USDA or CoFID measured values; tools/catalog in the source repository lists
  every change and its reference.

USDA FoodData Central: SR Legacy (April 2018) and Foundation Foods (April 2026)
  U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central.
  https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
  Public domain (CC0 1.0).

USDA FoodData Central: FNDDS 2021-2023 (October 2024)
  Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies, U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural
  Research Service. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/  Public domain (CC0 1.0).

UK Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (CoFID) 2021, McCance and Widdowson's
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid
  Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0:
  https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

MacroBase standard recipes
  Dishes worked out from the foods above (tools/catalog/recipes.csv in the source repository).

Open Food Facts: branded products sold in India
  Open Food Facts contributors, https://world.openfoodfacts.org/
  The branded product database in this app, databases/branded_foods.db, is made available under
  the Open Database Licence 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  Its individual contents are under the Database Contents Licence 1.0:
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/
  The file itself is in the app package (assets/databases/branded_foods.db), and the source repository
  holds it as tools/catalog/branded/branded_foods.csv with the scripts that build it.
  Products were chosen and checked by tools/catalog/import_branded_foods.py; values are as labelled.
